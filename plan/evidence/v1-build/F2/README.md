# F2: Renderer and simulator — evidence

Run 2026-09-26 on branch `v1-overnight`. Device checks ran against firmware
`c47e775e53`, flashed from that commit. Outputs: [run.log](run.log) (every
scenario on the board) and [perf.json](perf.json).

## What was built

- **Palette** (`render/palette.h`): "Warm Terminal", meaning black glass,
  oat eyes (#E8DCC4), one amber accent (#FFB000), a warm glow for cheers,
  a dim red for oops, and warm greys. The 256-entry table is computed at
  compile time: the bring-up colours, then 8-step ramps from black to each
  ink, and ramps from each eye tint down to the pupil.
- **Rasterizer** (`render/raster.*`): integer maths only. Shapes are spans
  on 4 sub-scanlines with 1/16 px precision (eyes, pupils, glints, rings),
  or vertical bands per sample column (mouth, squiggles), then a coverage
  level of 1 to 8 picks the ramp entry.
- **Fonts** (`render/font.*`, `firmware/assets/fonts.h`): Geist Mono
  (OFL, licence in `firmware/assets/OFL.txt`) at 13 px (8×18 cells) and
  22 px (13×30), with 4-bit coverage, printable ASCII plus "·". They are
  generated by `tools/fontgen/fontgen.py` from `landing/`'s node_modules.
- **Face** (`render/face.*`): a `Pose` sets eye openness, pupil position
  and size, upper lids with tilt, a happy lower-lid arc, a wink, squash and
  stretch, mouth curve, opening, width and offset, the face's offset and
  size, the glow and oops tints, and `raise` (moved up for the bubble). Any
  two poses blend field by field.
- **Animations** (`render/anim.*`): every name in BEHAVIORS.md §7 as a
  procedural timeline (cheer hops scale with size), the base looks (idle,
  working, asleep, no app, needs you by rung), and `Blend`: a 150 ms
  smoothstep from whatever was showing. A change in the middle of a blend
  starts from the current in-between pose.
- **Screens** (`render/screens.*`): the face with its bubble (squiggles and
  the amber word, placed by `at`), needs you (agent · project, "needs you on
  the Mac", "+N more"), threads (grouped by agent, most urgent first, with
  long names cut short), stats (name, level ring, days together) and the
  status strip (amber needs-you count, grey working count, and icons for no
  app, quiet, focus and low battery).
- **Device core** (`app/device.*`): parses `state` and `moment`, and picks
  the screen (pattern, then no app after 30 s, then threads or stats, then
  needs you, then face). The face follows a *source*: a moment, or a look
  from the state. Time-based changes (a moment ends, the app goes silent,
  the next rung) start their blends at the exact millisecond they happen,
  so a frozen clock gives identical frames. A tap on the strip cycles
  face → threads → stats. New: `dbg.reset`, plus `draw_us`/`push_us` in
  `dbg.ping`.
- **boopctl**: the scenario runner sends `dbg.reset` first; new commands
  `perf [--motion]` and `cam clip idle|needs_you|cheer`.

## Goldens accepted (after looking)

- `base`: idle (small smile), working (lids down, looking at the work,
  grey count), busy (smaller pupils), asleep (closed lines), no app (sleepy
  eyes, frown, plug icon), reconnect.
- `blend`: idle to working at 50, 100 and 150 ms, happy at 75 ms, an
  interruption at 75 ms that starts from the in-between pose, and the ease
  back after a moment ends.
- `moments`: cheer at sizes 1–3 (glow grows, hops grow) and every other
  animation at a characteristic frame.
- `mumble`: the word at the end and at the start, no word, a long word, and
  the bubble gone.
- `needs_you`: mid-blend, rungs 1–3 (leaning in more), "+1 more", a long
  project name cut with "..", and cleared.
- `screens`: threads, threads with 8 rows, threads empty, stats (40%, new,
  95%), face after the cycle, and strip icons (focus, quiet).
- `inputs`: the F1 placeholder golden is replaced by `face.png`.

## Problems found and fixed

- **2 fps at first.** The mouth tested 16 samples per pixel, each with a
  64-bit square root. Band rasterizing plus a 32-bit square root cut
  drawing from 70–136 ms to 5–11 ms per frame.
- **Dashed lid edges** where the pupil met a tilted lid; now the pupil
  darkens the soft edge instead.
- **The face jumped** up for the bubble (seen on the webcam). The face's
  place is now part of the pose (`raise`), so it eases.
- Scenario mistakes on my side: missing keep-alive `state`s let the
  no-app face in; timings were off by one message.

## Decisions (specs updated in the same commit)

- Integer-only drawing; Geist Mono fonts; `dbg.reset`; clips as live
  presets. All four are in the ARCHITECTURE.md §11 log. VERIFICATION.md §2–5
  and DEVICE.md §6 are updated.
- 40 MHz SPI is enough (44+ fps), so faster clocks weren't tried.
- **For F3:** moments currently play on the needs-you screen too. BEHAVIORS
  §1 says attention wins, so F3 should decide which moments may interrupt
  it. Idle life, gestures other than the strip tap, the 10 s threads
  timeout, dimming and mouth sync are F3 work. The device core's
  `advance`/`resync` is where F3's state machine should hook in.
