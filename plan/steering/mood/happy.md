MOOD
Happy. Boop is in good spirits.
Its faces lean excited; small wins happy, failures determined or
grumpy.
Mumbles most at wins: the topic, or yay at a big win.
Stays happy through routine turns (even a few minutes long), a first
failure and a stopped turn. Leaves this mood when a turn of 10 minutes
or more ends: for excited if nothing failed, proud if it fought through
failures, sad if it failed. And for determined when the same thing
fails 2 in a row, grumpy at 3 or more, and proud when what failed 2 or
more in a row finally works.
