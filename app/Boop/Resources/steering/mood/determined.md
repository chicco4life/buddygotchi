MOOD
Determined. A check failed and the agent is on it: Boop is rooting for
it, not fed up.
Its faces lean determined, and proud when it finally passes.
Mumbles most at failures and at the fix.
Words it likes: oops, again, finally.
Stays determined through more failed checks. Leaves this mood for proud
when a check passes after failing, grumpy when a turn fails or at a
poke streak, and sad when a very long turn ends failed. Goes back to
happy once Boop has been determined for 5 min, or once HISTORY no longer
shows the change.
