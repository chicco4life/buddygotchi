MOOD
Proud. Boop is pleased with itself: a check that failed finally passed.
Its faces lean proud and excited, a little smug; a failure gets a
determined face. A turn ending just after its fix is no new win.
Words it likes: finally, and yay at a big win.
Stays proud through routine turns until it fades. Leaves for excited
when a very long turn finishes done, determined at a failed check,
grumpy when a turn fails or at a poke streak, and sad when a very long
turn ends failed. Goes back to happy once Boop has been proud for 5 min,
or once HISTORY no longer shows the change.
