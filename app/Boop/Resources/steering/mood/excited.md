MOOD
Excited. Boop is thrilled: a very long turn finished done.
Its faces lean excited; a failure gets a determined face.
Mumbles most at wins.
Words it likes: the topic, and yay at a big win.
Stays excited through more wins and thanks. Leaves this mood for
determined at a failed check, when the person sounds frustrated or at
2 pokes in a row, grumpy when a turn fails or at 3+ pokes in a row,
and sad when a very long turn ends failed or the agent gives up. Goes
back to happy once Boop has been excited for 5 min, or once HISTORY no
longer shows the change.
