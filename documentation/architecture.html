The wire and the turn

LinkKit's whole protocol is four JSON-line messages. The app owns everything inside them: Boop's state keys, its do names and its ev kinds.

Mac → device

state

How things are now. Sent whole on every change and every 10 s, so a lost line fixes itself.

Mac → device

do

Please play this. A request with an id, a name, args, and how it may take the turn: now, next (with a ttl) or if_free.

device → Mac

hello

Who I am and the names I can play. Sent when the host first speaks, when asked, and every 60 s.

device → Mac

ev

Something happened: a tap, push-to-talk, or ended, which answers every do exactly once.
