# First live webcam verification — 2026-09-10

Tool workflow: **verified on the laptop camera and physical Buddy**.
Motion assessment: boop, hop and dance are visible through their transitions
and settling. This is a consecutive-frame review, not real-time video playback
or a complete firmware animation certification.

- Panic count unchanged (101 historical); bad-frame and parse-error counts stayed

## Observations

| Scenario | Visible interval in recording | Observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boop | onset ~1.23 s; heart gone ~2.2 s; overlay cleared ~2.83 s | Eye squish and recovery visible, one small pink heart moves and disappears; no repeated heart stream. |
| Working | ~4.23–7.2 s | Eye transition and small gaze shifts visible; capture scale is insufficient to certify exact gaze amplitudes. |
| Hop | onset ~7.23 s; settled by ~8 s | Arc eyes, one bounce and settling; no visible ring or particle burst. |
| Dance | onset ~11.23 s; particles disappear ~13.73 s; settled by ~14 s | Repeated sway/bounce with colored dots, returning to rest at approximately 2.5 seconds. |

No gross pause or stuck transition was apparent in these sequences. Some moving
eye edges appear doubled or partially scanned, e.g. around 11.27 s and 13.73 s.
These cannot be assigned to firmware from this recording alone: exposure,
rolling shutter and display refresh remain confounders. Exact particle count,
sub-frame smoothness, easing curves and small overshoot percentages are not
certified. No normal-speed playback was reviewed by the agent.

`STATE.connected` reflects a recent valid data frame (`dataConnected()`), not
exclusively a BLE connection. Its transition from false before injection to true
afterward is expected with USB injection and does not establish a competing
writer. No unexpected visible state replacements appeared in the reviewed span.
